Project Overview

// The idea behind Game-istry

Game-istry was created as an alternative, interactive way for students to learn chemistry experiments. It simulates a laboratory experience in which players collect apparatus and materials, follow experiment steps, and observe outcomes related to the rate of reaction.

The project focuses on Form 5 Chemistry, Chapter 1, and is designed for students around 16–17 years old in the science stream. The game combines 2D visuals, animation, text, and sound to guide players through the activities.

Learning Modules

// Three factors that affect reaction rate

01 · Catalyst

Explore how the presence of a catalyst affects the rate of a chemical reaction.

02 · Surface Area

Investigate how the size of a substance's surface area relates to reaction rate.

03 · Temperature

Explore how temperature affects the speed of a chemical reaction.

Gameplay & Mechanics

// Learn first, then test your understanding

Two Play Modes

  • Learning Mode: No countdown timer; players can work through the experiment while managing a health system.
  • Simulation Mode: Players complete experiment objectives within a time limit, with a single attempt per run.

Player Interaction

  • Move around the laboratory using the WASD keys.
  • Use mouse and keyboard input to interact with objects and events.
  • Collect the correct apparatus and materials, then follow the experiment sequence.
  • Complete objectives to progress through each module.

Feedback & Progress

  • Incorrect choices or steps can reduce player health in Learning Mode.
  • Simulation Mode uses a time limit to add a challenge.
  • Players receive a star rating based on remaining health or completion time, depending on the mode.
  • Completion screens provide a summary of the experiment's concept and outcome.

Development Elements

  • 2D laboratory environment and level layout.
  • Interactive objects, inventory and drag-and-drop-related systems.
  • Objective guidance, dialogue, and experiment-step progression.
  • Visual effects, animations, sound effects, and background music.
