// Dining room environment design

Environment & 3D Modeling

I worked on the dining room environment where the character has breakfast. My modeled assets included furniture, decorative objects, and other room details.

  • Dining table and chairs
  • Cabinet, door, and door handle
  • Wall paintings and decorative objects
  • Tabletop items such as cutlery, plates, a book, and a phone

Scene Setup & Visuals

I also contributed to the presentation of the dining room scene through lighting, materials, scene composition, and camera views.

  • Applied materials and textures to modeled objects
  • Set up Arnold mesh lighting for the room
  • Planned camera shots, including a medium shot and a point-of-view shot
  • Helped stage the breakfast scene within the group's cinematic story
